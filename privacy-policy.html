Privacy Policy — BEU Vision

Last updated: June 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how BEU Vision ("App", "we", "us", "our") handles information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy. Please read this policy carefully to understand our practices.

1. No Account Required

BEU Vision does not require you to create an account, sign up, or log in to use the App. We do not collect or store any personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or password through the App itself.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Since the App does not have account creation or login functionality, we do not directly collect:

  • Your name, email address, or contact details
  • Passwords or authentication credentials
  • Payment or billing information
  • Any personally identifiable information you would otherwise enter into a profile

3. Information Collected Automatically (via Third-Party Services)

While we do not operate our own data-collection systems tied to a user account, the App uses third-party services — primarily Google AdMob — to display advertisements. These third-party services may automatically collect certain information to function and to serve relevant ads, including:

  • Device information: device type, operating system version, device identifiers (such as Advertising ID)
  • Usage data: app interactions, ad impressions, and clicks
  • Approximate location: derived from IP address or device settings, used for ad targeting and fraud prevention
  • Advertising identifiers: such as Google Advertising ID (GAID), used to personalize or measure ads

This data is collected and processed by Google and its advertising partners in accordance with their own privacy policies, not by BEU Vision directly. We do not have access to, sell, or otherwise process this data ourselves beyond what is necessary to display ads within the App.

You can learn more about how Google collects and uses this data, and manage your ad personalization settings, here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps
  • Ads Settings (to opt out of personalized ads)

4. Analytics

If the App uses analytics tools (such as Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics) to understand general app usage patterns (e.g., number of installs, screens viewed, crash reports), this data is collected in aggregate and anonymized or pseudonymized form. It is used solely to improve app performance and user experience and is not linked to your identity.

5. Permissions

The App may request certain device permissions (such as internet access) solely to load content, display ads, and check for updates. We do not request permissions unrelated to the App's core functionality (such as contacts, camera, or microphone) unless a specific feature requiring them is clearly introduced and explained in the future.

6. Children's Privacy

BEU Vision is intended for general audiences, including students preparing for engineering admissions and competitive exams. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the App does not require account creation and does not collect personal information directly, no personally identifiable data is knowingly gathered from users of any age. If you believe a child has provided personal information through any third-party service integrated into this App, please contact us so we can address it.

7. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade any user data. The only data shared with third parties is the automatically collected advertising and analytics data described above, which is shared with:

  • Google AdMob (advertising)
  • Google Analytics / Firebase (if used, for app performance analytics)

These third parties operate under their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

8. Data Security

Although we do not store personal user data on our own servers (since there is no account system), we take reasonable measures to ensure the App itself is secure and free from harmful content. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of data handled by third-party services integrated into the App.

9. Your Choices

Since the App does not collect personally identifiable information directly:

  • You can limit ad personalization by adjusting your device's Ads Settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization, on Android devices).
  • You can reset your Advertising ID at any time through your device settings.
  • You can uninstall the App at any time, which will stop any further data collection by third-party services tied to that device session.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, app features, or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
